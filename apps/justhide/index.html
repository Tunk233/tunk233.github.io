JustHide is an app for hiding files on Android (including but not limited to photos, audio/video, and documents). After granting file-management permission, select the folders you want to hide and hide them in one tap. You can then conveniently browse media inside the app. App disguise and emergency exit help protect your privacy. It uses a special file-renaming mechanism for fast hiding, and hidden files can still be restored even if something goes wrong. Compared with encryption apps, hiding is fast and easy to use.

All processing stays on your device — we do not upload your files

Features

  • Hide / restore: Hide or restore files in selected folders, with support for append-hide.
  • Filename obfuscation: Local obfuscation makes files harder to recognize in system file managers.
  • Local preview: Browse hidden images, videos, audio, and documents in-app without fully restoring them first.
  • App disguise: Disguise the launcher icon as a calculator and enter the main UI via a specific gesture.
  • Emergency exit: When enabled, pressing Home, locking the screen, or switching apps closes in-app screens and removes the task from Recents.
  • Pro (optional): Unlock advanced features with a one-time purchase on Google Play.

Notes

  • On first launch you must accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and grant file-management permission.
  • Back up important files before hiding; mistakes or uninstalling the app may make recovery difficult.
  • This is a local utility — no cloud sync and no account login.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics (see Privacy Policy).

“JustHide”是一款用于隐藏安卓手机里的文件（包括但不限于照片、音视频、文档）的应用程序。用户授权文件管理权限后选择需要隐藏的文件夹，便可一键隐藏。隐藏之后可以在应用内方便的浏览媒体文件，应用伪装和应急退出功能可以很好的保护您的隐私。基于特殊的文件重命名机制实现快速隐藏，即便出现异常也能重新恢复隐藏的文件。相比其他文件加密软件，隐藏速度快，操作简单易用。

文件处理均在本机完成，不上传您的文件

主要功能

  • 一键隐藏 / 恢复：对选定文件夹中的文件进行隐藏或恢复，支持追加隐藏。
  • 文件名混淆：通过本地混淆处理降低文件在系统文件管理器中被直接识别的可能。
  • 本地预览：在应用内浏览已隐藏的图片、视频、音频与文档，无需先完整恢复。
  • 桌面伪装：可将应用图标伪装为计算器，通过特定操作进入主界面。
  • 应急退出：开启后，按 Home、锁屏或切换应用时可自动关闭页面并从最近任务中移除，降低被旁人查看的风险。
  • Pro（可选）：通过 Google Play 一次性内购解锁高级功能。

使用说明

  • 首次使用需同意《用户服务协议》与《隐私政策》，并授予文件管理权限。
  • 隐藏前请确认目标文件已备份；误操作或卸载应用可能导致找回困难。
  • 本应用为本地工具，不提供云端同步或账号登录。
  • 内购由 Google Play 处理；我们使用 Firebase Crashlytics 进行崩溃诊断（见隐私政策）。
