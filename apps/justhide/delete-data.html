Request Data Deletion

Last updated: July 28, 2026 · Effective: July 28, 2026

This page explains how you can delete or manage data related to JustHide (“App”). It is provided for Google Play and users who wish to exercise data-related rights.

Operator: Independent developer
Contact: support.won@gmail.com

1. What Data Exists

JustHide is a local utility. We do not operate user accounts or store your hidden file contents on our own servers.

  • On your device: hide-path settings, indexes, feature toggles, thumbnail cache, optional on-device logs, and locally stored Pro membership status (whether you have purchased Pro).
  • Google Play: If you buy Pro, payment and order records are processed and held by Google under your Google account.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): When the App crashes, diagnostic data (such as stack traces, App version, device model, and OS version) may be sent to Google Firebase to help us fix issues. This does not include the contents of files you hide. See the Privacy Policy for details.

2. Delete Data Yourself (Recommended)

  1. Hidden files: Restore hidden files or remove hide folders in the App before deleting App data, if you still need access to those files.
  2. App cache: Clear image/thumbnail cache in App settings where available.
  3. App data: In Android system settings, use “Clear storage” / “Clear data” for JustHide.
  4. Uninstall: Removes data in the App’s private storage. Uninstalling does not automatically restore files you hid on shared storage — restore them in the App first if needed.

Google Play orders: Manage or delete purchase history via your Google Account data & privacy settings and Google Play order history.

Crash reports: Data processed by Google Firebase is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

3. Request by Email

If you cannot perform the steps above or applicable law requires us to assist, email us. We will explain what can be deleted on-device versus what is held by Google, and respond within 30 days where reasonable.

Include: App name (JustHide), package name if known, device model (optional), and what you want deleted or corrected.

Email: support.won@gmail.com

4. What We Cannot Do Remotely

Because we do not host your files or a user database, we cannot remotely erase data on your phone or Google’s systems. Deletion of local App data is performed by you on the device; Google-held purchase and crash data is managed through Google’s tools and policies.

请求删除数据

本页说明如何删除或管理与本应用 JustHide（「本应用」）相关的数据，供 Google Play 及需要行使数据相关权利的用户查阅。

运营主体：个人开发者
联系邮箱：support.won@gmail.com

一、涉及哪些数据

本应用为本地工具，不提供用户账号，也不会在开发者自有服务器上存储您隐藏的文件内容。

  • 设备本地：隐藏路径与状态、功能开关、缩略图缓存、可选本机日志，以及本机保存的 Pro 会员是否已购买 等状态。
  • Google Play：若您购买 Pro，支付与订单由 Google 在您 Google 账户体系下处理与保存。
  • Firebase Crashlytics（Google）：应用崩溃时，可能向 Google Firebase 发送诊断信息（如崩溃堆栈、应用版本、设备型号、系统版本等），用于修复问题；不包含您隐藏文件的内容。详见《隐私政策》。

二、自行删除（推荐）

  1. 已隐藏文件：在清除应用数据或卸载前，如需继续使用，请先在应用内恢复隐藏或移除隐藏目录。
  2. 应用缓存：在应用设置中清除图片/缩略图缓存（如有该入口）。
  3. 应用数据：在系统设置中对 JustHide 执行「清除存储空间/清除数据」。
  4. 卸载应用：可删除应用私有目录中的数据；卸载不会自动恢复共享存储上已隐藏的文件，请先按需恢复。

Google Play 订单：可通过 Google 账户数据与隐私 及 Play 订单记录进行管理。

崩溃报告：由 Google Firebase 处理的数据适用 Google 隐私政策 与 Firebase 隐私说明。

三、邮件请求

若您无法自行操作，或适用法律要求我们协助，请发送邮件。我们将说明哪些数据可在本机删除、哪些由 Google 保存，并在合理期限内（一般不超过 30 日）答复。

建议注明：应用名称 JustHide、包名（如已知）、设备型号（可选）、希望删除或更正的内容。

发送邮件：support.won@gmail.com

四、无法远程删除的部分

因我们不托管您的文件或用户数据库，无法远程删除您手机上的数据或 Google 系统中的数据。本地数据需您在设备上操作；Google 侧的购买与崩溃相关数据请通过 Google 提供的工具与政策处理。
