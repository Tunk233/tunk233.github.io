Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026 · Effective: July 25, 2026

Welcome to FileHide (the “App”). We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the App. The App is distributed primarily via Google Play and similar channels. Please read carefully before use.

Operator: Independent developer
Contact: wonsupport@126.com

If mandatory laws in your country or region grant you stronger personal data protection rights, those rights prevail.

1. Information We Collect and Use

The App’s core features hide, restore, and preview files locally on your device. We do not upload the contents of files you choose to hide to any server, and we do not offer account registration or cloud sync.

To provide basic features, the App may process or store the following information locally on your device:

  1. Storage access: After you grant storage permission, the App accesses the folder paths and files you select in order to hide, restore, append-hide, and preview. Files remain on your device storage.
  2. Local settings and state: Including hidden folder paths, hide status, feature toggles (such as app disguise, emergency exit, theme), and consent status. This data is stored only on-device (e.g., local key-value storage) to restore your preferences.
  3. Local cache: Thumbnails and covers may be cached in the App’s private storage to improve browsing. You can clear them in the App.
  4. Optional logs: The App may generate on-device diagnostic logs for troubleshooting. Logs are not uploaded by default.

We do not collect sensitive personal information unrelated to core features (such as your legal name, government ID, contacts, or precise location), and we do not require an account.

2. Permissions

  • Storage / All files access: Required to read and modify files in folders you select for hide and restore. Denying this permission disables those features.
  • Internet: Mainly used to open this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service, or the app store page. The App does not use the network for ads or analytics.

You can manage these permissions in system settings. After revoking storage access, the App can no longer access the corresponding files.

3. Third-Party SDKs and Services

The current version does not integrate advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs that transmit user behavior or device identifiers to third parties.

Third-party components are mainly local libraries (e.g., local storage, image loading, media playback, permission helpers) that run on your device. If a future version adds networked third-party services, we will update this Policy and obtain consent where required.

4. Storage and Transmission

  • Path indexes, settings, and caches related to hiding are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not transmit your file contents to our servers.
  • When you open this Policy or the Terms in the in-app browser, the request is made from your device’s network environment; pages are hosted on GitHub Pages.

5. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We will not share, transfer, or publicly disclose personal information except:

  • with your clear consent; or
  • when required by applicable law, legal process, arbitration, or a competent authority.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended primarily for adults. If you are a minor, please use the App under a parent or guardian’s guidance. If we learn that a minor’s personal information was collected without appropriate consent, we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

You may manage related information by:

  • revoking storage or network permissions in system settings;
  • clearing image cache or removing hide-path settings in the App;
  • uninstalling the App to remove data in the App’s private storage (hidden files on device storage are not automatically restored by uninstalling — you must restore them yourself if needed).

If applicable law grants you rights such as access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing, you may email us and we will respond within a reasonable time. This Policy does not limit mandatory rights under your local law.

Contact: wonsupport@126.com

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect local App data. No measure is perfectly secure. File hiding reduces casual discovery; it is not a professional-grade encrypted vault. Please secure your device and back up important files.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page, and we may notify you in-app (for example via a consent dialog). Continued use means you acknowledge the updated Policy; if you disagree, please stop using and uninstall the App.

10. Contact Us

For questions or requests about this Policy or privacy, email wonsupport@126.com. We will reply within a reasonable time.

一、我们如何收集和使用信息

本应用的核心功能是在您的设备本地对文件进行隐藏、恢复与预览，不会将您选择隐藏的文件内容上传至服务器，也不提供账号注册或云端同步。

为实现基本功能，本应用可能在您的设备本地处理或保存以下信息：

  1. 存储访问相关信息：在您授权存储权限后，本应用需要访问您指定的文件夹路径及其中的文件，以便执行隐藏、恢复、追加隐藏与本地预览。文件本身仍保存在您的设备存储中。
  2. 本地配置与状态信息：包括但不限于隐藏文件夹路径、隐藏状态、功能开关（如桌面伪装、应急退出、主题等）、协议同意状态等。上述信息仅保存在本机（如本地键值存储），用于恢复您的使用设置。
  3. 本地缓存：为提升列表浏览体验，本应用可能在应用私有目录生成缩略图、封面等缓存数据，您可在应用内清除。
  4. 可选日志信息：在排查问题时，应用可能在本机生成运行日志。日志用于问题诊断，默认不会主动上传。

我们不会收集您的姓名、身份证号、通讯录、精确位置等与核心功能无关的敏感个人信息，也不会要求您注册账号。

二、权限说明

  • 存储权限 / 所有文件访问权限：用于读取、修改您指定目录下的文件，以实现隐藏与恢复。拒绝授权将导致文件隐藏相关功能无法使用。
  • 网络权限：主要用于在应用内打开本隐私政策、用户服务协议等网页，或跳转应用商店页面。本应用不因广告或统计分析而访问网络。

您可在系统设置中管理上述权限。撤回存储权限后，本应用将无法继续访问相应文件。

三、第三方 SDK 与服务

当前版本未接入广告、统计分析、崩溃上报等会向第三方传输用户行为或设备标识的 SDK。

本应用使用的第三方组件主要为本地功能库（例如本地存储、图片加载、媒体播放、权限申请等），其处理过程发生在您的设备本地。若后续版本引入需联网的第三方服务，我们将更新本政策并依法征得您的同意。
