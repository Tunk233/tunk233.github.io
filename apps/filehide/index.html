FileHide

文件隐藏

A local tool for hiding files on Android. After you choose folders and grant storage access, you can quickly hide photos, videos, text files, and more.

All processing stays on your device — we do not upload your files

Features

  • Hide / restore: Hide or restore files in selected folders, with support for append-hide.
  • Filename obfuscation: Local obfuscation makes files harder to recognize in system file managers.
  • Local preview: Browse hidden images, videos, audio, and documents in-app without fully restoring them first.
  • App disguise: Disguise the launcher icon as a calculator and enter the main UI via a specific gesture.
  • Emergency exit: When enabled, pressing Home, locking the screen, or switching apps closes in-app screens and removes the task from Recents.

Notes

  • On first launch you must accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and grant storage access.
  • Back up important files before hiding; mistakes or uninstalling the app may make recovery difficult.
  • This is a local utility — no cloud sync and no account login.

文件隐藏

FileHide

一款用于在安卓手机上本地隐藏文件的工具。选择文件夹并授权存储权限后，即可快速隐藏照片、视频、文本等文件。

文件处理均在本机完成，不上传您的文件

主要功能

  • 一键隐藏 / 恢复：对选定文件夹中的文件进行隐藏或恢复，支持追加隐藏。
  • 文件名混淆：通过本地混淆处理降低文件在系统文件管理器中被直接识别的可能。
  • 本地预览：在应用内浏览已隐藏的图片、视频、音频与文档，无需先完整恢复。
  • 桌面伪装：可将应用图标伪装为计算器，通过特定操作进入主界面。
  • 应急退出：开启后，按 Home、锁屏或切换应用时可自动关闭页面并从最近任务中移除，降低被旁人查看的风险。

使用说明

  • 首次使用需同意《用户服务协议》与《隐私政策》，并授予存储访问权限。
  • 隐藏前请确认目标文件已备份；误操作或卸载应用可能导致找回困难。
  • 本应用为本地工具，不提供云端同步或账号登录。
